**Caution by video**
- **SN10 (3):** the vehicle exploded minutes after landing. Set an end point or stop before that.
- **Flight 14 (2b):** the title says the ship exploded after splashdown. Prefer Flight 5 or Flight 13.
- **Flight 13 (2):** the booster's landing burn partly failed and it did not come down softly. Check the booster section before showing it.
- **Opportunity (11):** sad tone. Avoid the "Last Message from Mars" tribute videos.
- **Launch videos (1, 6):** loud. Warn before playing.

**Most recent successful flight:** Flight 14 is the most recent and the first to reach orbit, but SpaceX reported an engine failure on ascent and deorbited early. Flight 13 (24 Jul 2026) is the most recent with a clean ship splashdown. Sources disagree on Flight 14's date (22 vs 28 Sep).
