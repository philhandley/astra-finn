## Notes

- **Skipped (licence problems, as directed):** research items 4 (SpaceX Flickr, licence not verified), 5 (NASA HLS render credited to SpaceX, not public domain) and 24 (Chelyabinsk dashcam still, ESA Standard Licence).
- **mars-globe.jpg:** the listed source PIA02992 (Mars Digital Image Mosaic Globe) is two polar maps on a white background, not a globe disc, so it is not used. It is replaced by the NASA Hubble full-disc image PIA03154 (500 px), which matches the planet-disc look.
- **jupiter.jpg:** the source is only 400 px square, so the file is small. No larger full-disc Jupiter image was found in the NASA library.
- **Artist's concepts:** moon-basecamp.jpg and voyager.jpg are NASA artist's concepts, not photographs.
- **Wikimedia Commons licence fields:** the licence data was read through the Commons API. The Perseverance selfie has a blank Commons licence field and is treated as NASA public domain.
